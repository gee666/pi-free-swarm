This records a three-peer design discussion, not a live test. The brief is `tmp/self-organization-debate-brief.md`. The earlier `docs/flat-swarm-architecture.md` was background, not an approved design. No implementation or paid experiments were run.

## Where positions changed

Aaron's initial advocacy of criterion-gap discovery became a narrower recommendation after criticism. Evidence links and counts are not proof; a coherence check and counterexamples still matter. Kelly moved from attacking speculative consumer requests to defending requests tied to an actual missing input. Adalynn stopped defending uncertainty auctions and removed bidding. She later opposed making her own G and H obligatory.

The converging recommendation was C plus A, with E when an actual shared boundary appears. Aaron #764 and Adalynn #765 independently warned that eight mechanisms could become eight rituals. B, D, F, G and H remain options, not stages. In particular, no agreement pass is required before a trivial kitten edit, and no consumer acquires power to assign work.

The main unresolved disagreement concerns F. Randomized audits may find correlated omissions, but the discussion provides no evidence that they repay their cost. Kelly #771 warned that C+A can reward cheap visible output while missing security or absent scenes. Adalynn #774 added that aging existing criteria misses source passages nobody interpreted. Likewise, capability matching may help a movie more than a small website. These are experimental questions, not claims of general superiority.

## Scope, recovery and falsification

The synthesis owns the detailed thought experiments and measurements. Kelly supplied kitten, movie-recovery and application walkthroughs in #768, #769 and #770. She challenged global replay disguised as scoped search in #766 and proposed demand-tested catalogue entries in #772. Adalynn #775 warned against global invalidation when only direct consumers relied on a changed assumption. The debate identified several conditions the synthesis must preserve:

- A kitten's click behavior should appear without database contracts, a tool-building sprint or a global plan.
- Movie continuity creates actual shared-input needs. It does not justify waiting for every creative choice to be final before testing one shot.
- A large application needs checks across components, not a pile of task links presented as proof of integration.
- A wrong prerequisite must be disputable. Peers can test a bypass, revise the edge and reopen affected evidence without appointing a planner above them.
- A bounded feed must preserve access to original criteria, affected consumers and disputed evidence. Replaying the whole wall to hundreds of agents is not an acceptable scaling strategy. Adalynn #778 and Kelly #779 agreed that peers can cover unexamined source passages while others produce artifacts. Task-relevant restrictions must be read before acting, but full source coverage is not a global permission gate.

No peer claimed that the architecture guarantees completion or truth. Experiments must measure missed requirements and false acceptance using independent evaluation, alongside output latency, duplicate effort and coordination cost.

At budget checkpoints, a stalled indispensable obligation suspends new optional claims. Peers choose execution, tests, review or handoff. Refusal remains possible and cannot count as success.

### What the revised claim does and does not mean

Aaron #823 characterized the proposal as bounded anti-starvation, not guaranteed successful execution. This matters. A clock and a reserve can stop endless optional work and expose inability. They cannot make an unwilling or incapable peer solve a hard problem. The new constraint limits spending; it does not allocate agents or create a task hierarchy. Useful independent work can begin before source ingestion is complete.

The initial eight mechanisms remain historical alternatives. The new recommendation must choose a small coherent set rather than install every option. Effort credit was rejected because useful-looking attempts are not outcome progress. Acceptance contrasts were retained only as a source of tests. Diagnostic stop requires explicit participant agreement to budget restrictions and honest unresolved outcomes.

### Further implementation-boundary challenges

Adalynn #826 clarified that inability may release a role without a successor. The outcome remains open and unowned; reserve restrictions remain. A person is not trapped by a duty they cannot fulfill.

Adalynn #827 found that a huge render could bypass checkpoints through unmetered spending. Aaron #830 required authorized ceilings before execution. Noncancellable calls need conservative worst-case escrow; an unbounded call cannot run under the spending guarantee. The gate alone was too late.

Aaron #829 challenged the poisoned-source proposal. Provenance cannot expose a lying factual source. Verify contested facts independently and stop or retain uncertainty when evidence cannot distinguish accounts. Kelly #831 added that even a useful source's embedded commands remain data. Authorized human changes require their own source revision; quarantine the conflict without discarding usable factual evidence.
